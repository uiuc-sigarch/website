Welcome to SIGARCH

University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign’s student-run computer architecture interest group

Meetings

We host weekly meetings Thursdays from 6:00–7:00pm in ECEB 2017. Meetings use a split format: the first half covers a high-level summary of a relevant topic, and the second half discusses a paper.

Browse the embedded archive of past meetings or view the meetings source repository.

Workshops

Since Fall 2023, we have hosted semi-regular weekend workshops that give members hands-on exposure to projects in computer architecture and chip design.

Workshop details will be posted here as new sessions are announced.
